1. Executive Summary & Concept Statement
Chronic inflammatory disorders—including Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA), Psoriasis, Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD), and acute systemic sepsis—represent a formidable health and economic burden globally. Although biological therapeutics sequestering Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha (TNF-alpha) protein (e.g., infliximab, adalimumab) have reshaped clinical care, up to 40% of patients experience primary non-responsiveness, secondary loss of efficacy due to anti-drug antibody generation, or severe systemic immunosuppressive side effects. Meanwhile, small-molecule inhibitors targeting downstream kinase cascades (e.g., JAK/STAT pathways) frequently suffer from narrow therapeutic windows and multi-organ off-target toxicities.
eRNA-DEGRADE introduces a paradigm-shifting therapeutic strategy: targeting the non-coding regulome upstream of cytokine gene expression. Emerging evidence demonstrates that hyper-expression of TNF-alpha during acute and chronic inflammation is driven by specific super-enhancers (SEs) transcribing non-coding enhancer RNAs (eRNAs)—specifically human DHS44500 and its murine functional homolog, TNF-9. These short-lived eRNAs function as non-redundant structural scaffolds that anchor transcription factor assemblies, histone acetyltransferase coactivators (p300/CBP), RNA Polymerase II recruitment, and three-dimensional Enhancer-Promoter (E-P) chromatin loops.
Rather than attempting to neutralize circulating TNF-alpha protein after its synthesis and systemic release, eRNA-DEGRADE will design first-in-class Ribonuclease-Targeting Chimeras (RiboTACs) and synthetic RNA-binding small molecules to catalyze selective degradation of TNF-alpha super-enhancer eRNAs. By directing endogenous RNase L specifically to DHS44500 eRNA tertiary structural motifs, this approach will dismantle the super-enhancer biomolecular condensate, trigger regional E-P chromatin loop collapse, and silence pathologically elevated TNF-alpha gene transcription at its genomic origin in disease-associated macrophages and primary patient immune cells, without causing generalized immunosuppression.
2. Statement of Innovation & Paradigm Shift
2.1 Disruptive Conceptual Breakthrough
Standard pharmacological paradigms target catalytic active sites or ligand-binding pockets on proteins. eRNA-DEGRADE shifts the intervention node upstream to non-coding RNA scaffolds that orchestrate nuclear gene transcription:
Epigenomic Condensate Dissolution: Rather than inhibiting individual protein-protein contacts, RiboTAC-mediated destruction of DHS44500 eRNA dismantles the phase-separated super-enhancer condensate, terminating transcription factor re-loading before pre-mRNA synthesis can occur.
Cell-State Selectivity: Unlike ubiquitously expressed signaling proteins or housekeeping transcripts, eRNAs are transcribed exclusively in specific disease-activated cell states (e.g., LPS/TLR4-stimulated inflammatory macrophages). RiboTACs targeting eRNAs achieve cell-state specificity unapproachable by classic small-molecule kinase inhibitors.
Catalytic Sub-Stoichiometric Mechanism: RiboTACs operate catalytically; a single degrader molecule can induce the sequential cleavage of multiple target eRNA transcripts, overcoming the stoichiometric limitations of occupancy-based small-molecule antagonists.
2.2 Technological & Methodological Innovation
eRNA-DEGRADE integrates several emerging frontiers into a cohesive drug discovery platform:
AI/ML RNA Scaffold Engineering: Deployment of 2.5D/3D RNA structural predictions, deep-learning docking pipelines, and scaffold-aware generative models trained specifically on RNA-small molecule binding landscapes.
DEL Screening against Dynamic Non-Coding RNA: Application of DNA-Encoded Libraries (>10^8 diverse chemical entities) to screen natively folded eRNA hairpins under competitive physiological conditions.
Single-Cell Off-Target Profiling via drug2cell: Adaptation of single-cell multi-omic frameworks to map eRNA target availability across millions of human single-cell transcriptomes, creating a computational safety screen before animal studies.
Epigenomic Architecture Tracking: Direct measurement of 3D chromatin loop decay using high-resolution Micro-C, single-nucleus snm3C-seq, and nascent transcription tracking via PRO-seq.
2.3 Intellectual Property Strategy & Translational Roadmap
This proposal establishes a structured pipeline for intellectual property (IP) protection and clinical translation:
Patent Portfolio Generation: IP filings will encompass (a) composition-of-matter claims for synthetic RiboTAC structures targeting DHS44500, (b) diagnostic method patents utilizing DHS44500 eRNA expression as a stratification biomarker, and (c) platform claims for machine learning-guided eRNA degrader identification.
Commercialization & Spin-Off Trajectory: Project milestones are aligned to support an ERC Proof of Concept (PoC) grant application and the establishment of a biotechnology spin-off focused on regulome-targeted therapeutics for autoimmune and inflammatory diseases.

4. Work Packages and Research Methodology
Work Package 1: Structural Architecture & Dynamics of DHS44500 / TNF-9 eRNAs
Lead Focus: High-resolution structural characterization of human DHS44500 eRNA and mouse TNF-9 eRNA.
1.1 RNA Structure Mapping: Perform SHAPE-MaP (Selective 2'-Hydroxyl Acylation analyzed by Primer Extension and Mutational Profiling) and dMS-MaPseq on in vitro-transcribed and endogenously isolated DHS44500 eRNA under physiological ionic conditions. Resolve stable secondary motifs (hairpin loops, internal bulges, triple-helix nodes).
1.2 High-Resolution Structural Determination: Subject structured sub-domains to NMR Spectroscopy and single-particle Cryo-EM to capture dynamic 3D tertiary conformations.
1.3 Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulations: Utilize ensemble-based MD simulations (using AMBER/GROMACS with RNA-specific force fields) to model structural flexibility, binding pocket exposure, and hydration shells.
Work Package 2: AI/ML-Guided Design, DEL Screening, and RiboTAC Chemical Synthesis
Lead Focus: Ligand discovery and synthetic chemistry of catalytic heterobifunctional RNA degraders.
2.1 Machine Learning & Computational Docking: Deploy deep learning models (e.g., Ribosphere, RNAincoder, RNA language models, SMARTBind, GATRsite) to screen small-molecule libraries against 3D RNA motifs identified in WP1. Predict binding free energy, target selectivity over ribosomal/transfer RNAs, and drug-like properties (Bemis-Murcko scaffolds).
2.2 DNA-Encoded Library (DEL) Selection: Construct and screen an RNA-focused DNA-Encoded Library (>10^8 diverse small molecules) against immobilized folded DHS44500 eRNA constructs under competitive conditions.
2.3 RiboTAC Design & Medicinal Chemistry: Conjugate validated small-molecule eRNA binders to a synthetic RNase L recruiter (e.g., a low-molecular-weight 2'-5' oligoadenylate monomer mimic or heterocyclic recruiter) via systematic PEG or alkyl linker libraries. Optimize PK properties, cell permeability, and chemical stability.
Work Package 3: Biophysical Validation and drug2cell Single-Cell Off-Target Profiling
Lead Focus: Cell-free biophysical binding assays and single-cell resolution safety profiling.
3.1 Biophysical Binding & Catalytic Cleavage Assays: Quantify binding kinetics (Kd, kon, koff) via Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR) and MicroScale Thermophoresis (MST). Confirm target engagement in living cells via Chem-CLIP (Chemical Crosslinking and Isolation by Pull-down) and target RNA degradation kinetics via RT-qPCR and RNA-seq.
3.2 Adaptation of the drug2cell Single-Cell Pipeline: Integrate scRNA-seq datasets from clinical cohorts of RA, psoriasis, and healthy controls. Substitute standard protein targets in the drug2cell pipeline with custom eRNA binding scores to map RiboTAC target expression across single-cell clusters.
3.3 Safety Guardrail: Confirm zero expression of DHS44500/TNF-9 eRNA targets in non-immune cell populations (e.g., cardiac sinoatrial pacemaker cells, blood-brain barrier endothelial cells, renal podocytes) to ensure a high therapeutic index.
Work Package 4: Deep Mechanistic Decoupling & In Vivo Preclinical Validation
Lead Focus: Epigenomic impact evaluation, mouse model efficacy, and human primary immune cell translation.
4.1 Epigenomic Condensate & Chromatin Architecture Profiling: Evaluate the collapse of enhancer-promoter (E-P) loops via high-resolution Micro-C and single-nucleus snm3C-seq. Track nascent transcriptional elongation and pausing dynamics at the TNF-alpha locus using PRO-seq. Visualize phase-separated super-enhancer condensate disruption (p300, BRD4, MED1) using super-resolution microscopy (STED/SIM).
4.2 In Vivo Preclinical Efficacy in Mouse Models: Administer optimized TNF-9-targeting RiboTACs in established mouse models of chronic inflammation:
Collagen-Induced Arthritis (CIA) (Rheumatoid arthritis model).
Imiquimod-Induced Psoriasis.
LPS-Induced Hyper-Inflammatory Sepsis.
Benchmark therapeutic efficacy against anti-TNF antibody standard-of-care (e.g., clinical scoring, joint histology, systemic cytokine profiling via multiplex ELISA).
4.3 Translational Validation in Patient-Derived PBMCs: Validate target eRNA depletion, TNF-alpha gene silencing, and suppression of downstream inflammatory mediators (IL-1beta, IL-6) in primary PBMCs and synovial fluid macrophages isolated from patients with active RA and psoriasis.

6. Risk Management & High-Risk / High-Gain Assessment
High-Risk Factor 1: Transient eRNA Half-Life & Low Abundance
Mitigation Strategy: RiboTACs act catalytically; one molecule degrades multiple eRNA transcripts. Targeted catalytic delivery destroys the structural scaffold before transcription re-initiates.
High-Risk Factor 2: Dynamic Conformational Flexibility of Non-Coding RNA
Mitigation Strategy: Ensemble structural determination (SHAPE-MaP + Cryo-EM + MD simulations). Screening targets structurally rigid tertiary hairpins and triple helices.
High-Risk Factor 3: Off-Target Degradation of Endogenous Structured RNAs
Mitigation Strategy: Multi-stage computational counter-screening against human transcriptome motifs. Perform custom drug2cell single-cell mapping across non-immune tissue cell types to ensure cell-type specific engagement.
7. Feasibility, Key Resources & Methodological Synergy
Proof-of-Concept Baseline: Deleting the TNF-9 enhancer region in mice (Tnf-9-/-) significantly reduces Tnf-alpha expression, mitigates arthritis, and protects against LPS-induced septic shock without systemic pathology.
ASO Preclinical Validation: Knockdown of mouse TNF-9 eRNA and human DHS44500 eRNA using Antisense Oligonucleotides (ASOs) suppresses TNF-alpha expression, proving the causal regulatory dependency on the eRNA transcript.
Chemical Feasibility: The RiboTAC strategy has been validated in human oncology models (e.g., MYC mRNA degrader, junctional lncRNA degraders), establishing clear synthetic pathways for recruiting endogenous RNase L via bifunctional small molecules.
Single-Cell Safety: Combining single-cell genomics with drug2cell analytical pipelines enables precise spatial and cell-type toxicity screening prior to extensive animal studies.
