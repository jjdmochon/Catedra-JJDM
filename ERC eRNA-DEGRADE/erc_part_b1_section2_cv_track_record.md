### Personal Statement & Scientific Vision

Regarding my research career over the past two decades, I have focused on building chemical tools to interrogate, label, and manipulate nucleic acids in living systems. My work sits at the intersection of synthetic organic chemistry, dynamic chemical probes, and bio-nanotechnology. From my early work at Edinburgh developing Dynamic Chemical Labelling (DCL) and Peptide Nucleic Acid (PNA) architectures to our recent developments in PCR-free microRNA sensing, cell-barcoding nanocatalysts, and synthetic CRISPR-Cas13 tools, I have maintained a clear focus on turning fundamental chemical insights into robust molecular platforms.

On the translational and entrepreneurial front, I have always believed that fundamental chemical innovations should reach real-world clinical and industrial applications. I co-founded DestiNA Genomics Ltd to commercialize our PNA dynamic chemical labelling technology, securing over €8M in public and private funding, establishing international industrial partnerships, and building a granted patent portfolio of 16 patent families (including granted US patents `US11242526B2` and `US8716457B2`). This project, eRNA-DEGRADE, represents the natural evolution of my research vision: taking our 20-year foundation in nucleic acid chemical recognition and applying it to sub-stoichiometric catalytic RiboTAC degraders targeting non-coding enhancer RNA scaffolds.
