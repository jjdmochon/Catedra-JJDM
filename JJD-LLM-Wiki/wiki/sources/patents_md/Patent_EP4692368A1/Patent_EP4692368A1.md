## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes a multiplexed biodetection system that combines optical measurement platforms with dynamic chemical labelling for the highly sensitive detection of target biomarkers, such as microRNAs and proteins. It provides improved diagnostics by reducing background noise and multiplexing measurements on a single sample.
