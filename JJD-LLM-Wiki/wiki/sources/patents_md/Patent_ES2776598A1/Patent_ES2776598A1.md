## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes multifunctional nanoparticles designed for teragnosis (theranostics). The polymer-based nanoparticles are engineered to encapsulate therapeutic oncology drugs and carry fluorescent tags, enabling simultaneous targeted cancer therapy and real-time visualization of drug distribution.
