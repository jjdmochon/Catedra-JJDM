## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes the 'CRISPNA' platform, which combines CRISPR-Cas genome editing tools with Peptide Nucleic Acids (PNA). This hybrid platform achieves superior target specificity and editing efficiency, minimizing off-target genomic cleavage and improving diagnostics.
