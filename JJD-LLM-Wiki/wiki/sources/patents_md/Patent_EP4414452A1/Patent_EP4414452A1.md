## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes a novel dual-guide RNA composition that improves the specificity of CRISPR-Cas13 systems. By utilizing two distinct RNA guides (dcrRNA and dtracrRNA), the system significantly enhances target mismatch discrimination, enabling precise single-nucleotide variant (SNV) detection for molecular diagnostics.
