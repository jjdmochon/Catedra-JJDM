## 📄 Abstract
This patent details a diagnostic method to detect and profile viruses and bacteria (such as SARS-CoV-2) inside circulating epithelial cells isolated from peripheral blood. It acts as a biomarker platform for tracking severe inflammatory or infectious disease states and monitoring patient recovery.
