## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes purine-derived chemical compounds that act as selective inhibitors of Death-Associated Protein Kinase 1 (DAPK-1). The compounds are designed as pro-apoptotic inducers for oncology treatments, selectively targeting leukemic lymphocytes and cancer cells.
