## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes a method for the single-molecule detection and quantification of microRNAs with single-base specificity. Using abasic Peptide Nucleic Acids (PNA) and dynamic chemical labelling on single-molecule arrays (Simoa), it enables sensitive diagnosis of drug-induced liver injury and other clinical conditions.
