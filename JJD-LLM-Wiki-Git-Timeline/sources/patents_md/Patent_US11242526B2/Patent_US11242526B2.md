## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes an improved abasic Peptide Nucleic Acid (PNA) probe with optimized backbone structures and sequence lengths. The probe is engineered to bind specifically to target microRNAs or SNPs, providing superior single-nucleotide mismatch discrimination in chemical-labeling assays.
