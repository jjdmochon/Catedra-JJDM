## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes multifunctional nanoparticles for theragnosis, optimizing the co-delivery of chemotherapeutic agents and imaging agents (like magnetic or fluorescent trackers) to cancer cells, with controlled-release kinetics triggered by local physiological conditions.
