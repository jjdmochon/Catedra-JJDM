## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes methods and devices for isolating circulating cells of epithelial origin (circulating tumor cells or CTCs) from peripheral blood. Using specialized polymer microfilters and microfluidic channels, the technology allows for clean isolation of rare cells for downstream analysis and liquid biopsy.
