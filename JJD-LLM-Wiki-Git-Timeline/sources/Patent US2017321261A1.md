## 📄 Abstract
This patent details a diagnostic method for detecting circulating tumor cells (CTCs) exhibiting both epithelial and mesenchymal-to-epithelial transition (EMT) phenotypes. The method utilizes microRNA-21 as a highly specific biomarker to track cancer progression and metastasis.
