## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes a methodology for growing pharmaceutically active protein crystals (such as human insulin) in-situ within a short-peptide supramolecular hydrogel. The composite hydrogel increases the thermal stability of the protein crystals and modulates their in vivo release profiles for sustained drug delivery.
