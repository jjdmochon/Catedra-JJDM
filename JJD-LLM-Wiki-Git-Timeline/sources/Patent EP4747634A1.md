## 📄 Abstract
This patent describes surface-functionalized nanoparticles optimized for targeted drug delivery and high-contrast bioimaging. The functionalized nanoparticles are designed to target cancer cells specifically, delivering diagnostic markers or therapeutic agents (theranostics) directly to tumor cells with reduced systemic toxicity.
